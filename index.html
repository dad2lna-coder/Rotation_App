📌 Core Problem

Current Process
Manual email forwards.
The Gap
Not everyone gets the memo. Key operational teams lack visibility on who is gone, where they are, and when they will return.
Target Audience
Scheduling, Payroll, Coordination Center, TSMs, Finance, Training, and Senior Management.

🎯 Goals and Objectives

Goals

  • Improve visibility when personnel move out of the operation.
  • Reduce manual forwarding and delayed notifications.
  • Standardize required movement notification fields.
  • Ensure impacted teams receive timely, accurate information.
  • Create a repeatable notification model for future movement types.

Objectives

  • Identify the upstream sender for each movement type.
  • Confirm required recipients and distribution lists.
  • Define standard departure, duration, return, and location fields.
  • Determine advance notice requirements by team.
  • Identify automation opportunities using distribution lists, forms, or Power Automate.

Daily workflow

  1. Open OperationalMovements.exe from OneDrive - USTSA\\FACTTT.
  2. The app greets you and Refresh-loads data/dashboard.json.
  3. Edit ideas, actions, questions, and notes.
  4. Select Save. That updates data/dashboard.json and writes a copy to inbox/.
  5. Power Automate merges that file into data/dashboard.json, posts Teams, and moves it to archive/.
  6. Select Refresh later to pick up merges from other operators.
