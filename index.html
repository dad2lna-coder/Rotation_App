Daily workflow

  1. Open LetThemCook.exe from OneDrive - USTSA\\FACTTT.
  2. The app greets you and Refresh-loads data/initiatives.json.
  3. Edit ideas, actions, questions, and notes.
  4. Select Save. That updates data/initiatives.json and writes a copy to inbox/.
  5. Power Automate merges that file into data/initiatives.json, posts Teams, and moves it to archive/.
  6. Select Refresh later to pick up merges from other operators.
